# Why the exhaustive check proves the claim

Suppose 30 six-element blocks cover every triple of a 15-point set. Each pair
lies in at least four blocks: these must cover its 13 other points, four at a
time. The link at any point therefore has at least `ceil(14 × 4 / 5) = 12`
blocks. There are `30 × 6 = 15 × 12` point incidences, so every point lies in
exactly 12 blocks. Repeated blocks would contradict the same lower bound
after removing a copy. At each point, the total pair multiplicity above four
is therefore exactly `12 × 5 − 14 × 4 = 4`.

Each point link is consequently a 12-block pair covering by five-element
subsets of 14 points. The supplied `links_14_5_12.g6` lists its 28,162
isomorphism classes, one bipartite incidence graph per line. Its SHA-256 is

Relabel that point as 0 and its link as the catalogue representative. Every
